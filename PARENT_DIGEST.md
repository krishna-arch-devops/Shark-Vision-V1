# SHARKVISION Parent Weekly Progress Digest — Iteration 5

## 1. Overview
The **Parent Weekly Digest** (`/parent/progress`) delivers an honest, plain-English summary of candidate examination consistency, accuracy growth, and remedial task follow-through without technical jargon.

## 2. Digest Components
- **Reporting Metrics**: Total completed tests, remedial revision tasks finished, and preparation rhythm assessment.
- **Narrative Observations**: Objective description of test effort and completion.
- **Remedial Focus**: Highlights verified completion of practice tasks assigned in identified topic weakness areas.
- **Privacy & Child Isolation**: Accessible exclusively by parents with verified relationships (`parent_student_links.is_verified = true`).
